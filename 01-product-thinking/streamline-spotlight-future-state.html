Future-State Journey · Persona: The Disillusioned Enthusiast

StreamLine Spotlight

For high-value viewers, we will launch StreamLine Spotlight — a premium, highly-curated space for quality cinema — to restore brand authority and win back users lost to niche, human-curated competitors before churn accelerates further.

Sentiment Arc — Friction to Delight

Advocacy Moment

Renews subscription and recommends Spotlight to like-minded friends.

1

Rediscovery

Action → Benefit Notices in-app editorial pick → distinct signal, not algorithmic guesswork.
Internal State Skepticism softens into cautious curiosity.
Pain Point Addressed Distrust of repetitive "Because you watched" suggestions.
2

The Match

Action → Benefit Watches curator's hidden-gem pick → finds a match beyond his usual genre.
Internal State Surprise and delight; feels seen as a person, not a genre.
Pain Point Addressed Franchise-driven, low-diversity recommendations.
3

The Switch

Action → Benefit Checks Spotlight before texting a friend → gets consistent curated picks in-app.
Internal State Growing reliance on platform curation over external sources.
Pain Point Addressed Dependence on friends and a competitor's curated email.
4

Renewed Loyalty

Action → Benefit Renews subscription → recommends Spotlight to like-minded friends.
Internal State Restored trust; platform reclaims taste-making authority.
Pain Point Addressed Churn risk to niche, human-curated competitors.

Competitive Advantages Over the Manual Workaround

1

On-demand, in-app curation beats waiting on a competitor's fixed weekly email schedule.

2

Combines full catalog scale with human-level taste, which a single friend's opinion cannot offer.

3

Curation improves over time using viewing data, unlike a friend's or a newsletter's static picks.
